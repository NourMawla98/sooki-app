Find your bra size in 3 steps
Step 1
Measure your band
Wrap a tape around your ribcage, directly under your bust. Breathe out and measure in cm. Round up to the nearest 5 (e.g. 78 cm → 80).
Step 2
Find your cup size
Measure around the fullest part of your bust. Subtract your band from this number to find the cup difference.
